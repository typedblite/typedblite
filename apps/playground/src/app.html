Press Ctrl or ⌘ + Enter to run. Each run starts from an empty database. In the editor, Ctrl + Space completes, F2 renames, F12 goes to a definition, and Shift + Alt + F formats. Esc then Tab leaves the editor.

A statement that follows a write or a query ends with end;. In TypeQL 3, a match after an insert is the next stage of the same pipeline, not a new query.

Everything runs in your browser, in a worker, on WASM SQLite. Your program is not sent anywhere, and a shared link holds the whole program after the #.
